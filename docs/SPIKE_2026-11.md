# Phase 1 data spike: public share-pledge exposures (US / UK)

Date: 2 Nov 2026 · Scope: can public filings supply real edges for the exposure graph?

## Verdict

**Gate passed on the US side; the UK side needs a different seed.**

| Gate | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Borrower -> stock edges | ~150 real | **397 extracted, ~84% precision → ~330 real**, across 341 issuers |
| Borrower -> lender edges | ~30 real | **61 borrower-stock pairs with a named lender, ~65% precision → ~40 real** |

## 1. SEC EDGAR (US)

- Full-text search, 2024-2025, nine pledge phrases, forms DEF 14A and Schedule 13D (old and new form codes).
- **2,539 documents** downloaded (1,656 proxy, 883 13D); only pledge sentences kept (`data/edgar_passages.jsonl`).
- Rule-based extraction (`scripts/edgar_extract.py`):
  - 427 proxies and 277 13Ds contain an actual pledge (after removing policy text and hedge-fund "may be pledged" boilerplate).
  - **397 holder -> stock edges**, 273 with a share count, 151 with a named holder.
  - **111 lender mentions / 56 distinct lenders** (Citi, JPMorgan, BofA, UBS, Raymond James, RBC, Morgan Stanley, Axos, regional banks).
- Hand-checked samples: 21/25 holder-stock edges real; ~10/15 lender edges real.

Notable real edges (all from public filings):
- Carlos Slim group: 331.5M America Movil B shares pledged
- LGC Holdco (Murdoch family): 30.4M News Corp and 37.0M Fox Class B shares pledged
- PACS Group CEO: 9.5M shares, margin loan with UBS as agent
- Grindr: Longview vehicles pledged 26.6M and 35.4M shares to lenders
- Woodbridge (Thomson family) -> Thomson Reuters shares pledged to RBC
- UWM (Mat Ishbia vehicle) -> JPMorgan; Amneal (Patel family) -> UBS, 19M shares

Known weaknesses (fix in the build, not the spike):
- **Holder names**: 62% unresolved because "Mr. Smith" is split at the period, and proxy footnotes refer to a table row. Fix: better sentence splitter + table-row linking, or LLM extraction.
- Lender precision ~65%: generic "X Bank" matches in long 13D legal text.
- Some edges are historic (loan repaid) and need a status field.

## 2. Companies House (UK)

- REST key works (charges, profiles, search, document API).
- **Random sample of holding companies (SIC 64209/64205)**: of 300 checked, 60 file full/group accounts, 24 have charges, 2 have share charges, and those are over *private subsidiary* shares (e.g. Medco Energi's Thai subsidiary to BNP Paribas / Standard Chartered). Listed-stake yield ≈ 0. **Random sampling is the wrong approach.**
- `has_charges` on the company profile is unreliable (Tesco shows False with 9 charges); always call `/charges`.
- **Cross-link from SEC holders**: 21 UK-looking names, 6 matched by name, 0 with charges; several matches are same-name companies, not the holder. Cross-border holders in US filings are mostly offshore (Cayman/BVI) rather than UK-registered.
- Charge descriptions are often "see image for full details": the useful detail is in the PDF (document API), which would need PDF text extraction.

**What would work in the UK**: seed from the listed side, not the holdco side.
1. FCA National Storage Mechanism (UK MAR PDMR notices mention pledges; TR-1 major-holding notices name holders). The API host `api.data.fca.org.uk` is reachable but the search index is undocumented, so it needs reverse-engineering.
2. Then look up those named holders in Companies House and read the charge PDFs.

## Recommendation

- Build the graph's **US core from EDGAR now**: it passes the gate.
- Treat UK as **Phase 2b**: an FCA NSM scraper, then Companies House charge PDFs, for the London holdco layer.
- Fill the remaining lender edges with clearly labelled synthetic syndicates.
